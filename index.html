How the kits work

If you can copy and paste, you can use this.

Built for church teams, often part-time staff and volunteers, who want to use AI to save time without losing the heart of their church. You don't need to be a tech person.

1

Set up your Church Voice

Fill out the Church Voice prompt once and save the reply, so every draft sounds like your church.

2

Pick the prompt

Each one has a title, when to use it, the copy-paste prompt and a quick tip.

3

Fill in the [BRACKETS]

Dates, names, links, details. The more specific you are, the better the result.

4

Paste, read, refine

"Make it shorter." "Warmer." "Less churchy." "Give me three more options."

5

Verify before you post

AI is a fast first-draft partner, not a final approver. People check everything.

Works in ChatGPT (the free version handles most prompts), Claude, Google Gemini and Microsoft Copilot.

Included in every kit

Verify Before You Post

AI tools are helpful, fast, and confident, even when they're wrong. Before anything AI-assisted goes out under your church's name, run it through this checklist.

AI drafts, people decide.

  • Facts and logisticsEvery date, time, address, deadline and link.
  • Names and titlesSpellings, staff titles, and consent to be named.
  • Scripture and quotesYour translation, the right reference, real sources.
  • Privacy and sensitive dataNo prayer requests, kids' info or giving records.
  • Stories and testimoniesWritten permission, and let them review it.
  • Theology and toneA pastor reviews anything touching doctrine.
  • ImagesOdd hands, garbled text, wrong logos. Never passed off as real photos.
  • AccessibilityAlt text, captions, readable sizes and contrast.
  • Brand checkRight logo, colors, fonts and terms.
  • The human readRead it out loud. Does a real person talk like this?
